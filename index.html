Yu Guan (管羽)

I am a senior undergraduate student at the Department of Electronic Engineering, Tsinghua University (Class of 无23). I am currently taking a gap year to focus on research and will graduate in 2027.

I am an Undergraduate Research Assistant at Beijing Galbot Co., Ltd. and the Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS) at Tsinghua University, where I am advised by Prof. Li Yi. Previously, I was a Robotics Engineering Intern at BYD Co., Ltd., focusing on dual-arm workstation robots and Vision-Language-Action (VLA) studies.

My research focuses on humanoid robot motion control, Embodied AI, World Action Models (WAM), and Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models. When processing data for motion tracking, I strongly believe in a "Less is More" philosophy, prioritizing high-quality representation.
